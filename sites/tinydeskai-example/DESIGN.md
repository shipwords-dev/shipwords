# Tiny Desk AI design direction

- **Audience**: people coming from the TikTok "Outstanding" meme who want to put the coach who lost this week, a rival team, a favorite player or themselves at the desk (celebrities first, friends second), then post the clip to TikTok and group chats. Not NPR Tiny Desk listeners, so nothing quiet or arty.
- **Mood**: live footage first, with a bit of gig-poster playfulness. Real frames are the hero and text only explains them; warm, not cold or techy.
- **Fonts**: headings Fraunces (heavy serif); short poster labels (crew names, section tags, ticket stubs) Anton (condensed bold, uppercase, never for sentences or the H1); body Instrument Sans; hard numbers (price, duration, seats) JetBrains Mono.
- **Colors**: stage black `#0f0c0a`, panel `#1a1511`; cream text `#efe7da`; stage-light amber `#f2a33a` for primary buttons and emphasis; record red `#e5483a` only for small accents like `● REC` and "live"; poster paper `#efe6d2` with ink `#1d1712`. Dividers are cream at 12% opacity.
- **Texture**: a faint film grain on dark sections; sample clips are muted, autoplay, loop, `object-fit: cover`, 14px radius; dark and poster-paper sections alternate with generous spacing.
- **Home first screen**: left, a `● REC` tag, a large serif H1, one subtitle and the generator (stage photo); right, a 9:16 sample card with a three-option pill above it, "Dogs / The viral original / Made here" (selected one amber): it defaults to our dog version (five AI-generated dogs; every competitor shows people, so pets set us apart; it has the original audio, muted by default, with a sound toggle top right) and can switch to the top viral TikTok (the coaching staff) or a real all-five-swapped output (AI-generated faces, said under the card). The stage follows the switch, so on load it shows the dogs like the sample. All three share one player, and the sound toggle survives the switch. The card has a crew tag, a one-line hook below and a mono like count or how it was made. On mobile: title, viral sample, generator. The generate button is always enabled: with no photo of your own yet it opens the lead's file picker; without the consent box ticked it highlights the box. Below the first screen: a viral wall of three vertical cards (two viral TikToks and one of our AI samples), each with a one-line hook, then a row of the four shots (band, lead laughing, sax, band finale), each labelled with its timestamp and who is swapped.
- **Avoid**: NPR branding in the site's own identity (logo, wordmark, colors): no NPR logo, red and black, or Tiny Desk wordmark. The output keeps the NPR original footage as its base; that is a product decision, not branding. No blue primary buttons, purple gradients or glassmorphism, the usual AI tool site look; no neon yellow on black or all-caps outlined headlines (the tinydeskai.live and Higgsfield style); not the template's light grey background. The first screen and viral wall use the original viral TikToks (with real coaches and star players, decided 2026-10-27), cut at the end of the performance, without the tutorial ad at the end or other sites' watermarks; product samples like the shot strip still use AI-generated musicians.

## Components

- **Sample card (first screen)**: 9:16, no rotation or white border, thin outline and a large shadow. Anton crew tag top left (such as `THE OFFICE BAND`), a mono strip at the bottom: `sample · AI cast · about 18 s · 720p`.
- **Shot strip**: four small vertical samples side by side (horizontal scroll on mobile), each with a mono timestamp, shot name and who is swapped.
- **Upload stage**: no vertical upload boxes. No single frame of the viral original shows all five musicians, so the stage is a three-frame collage `public/media/stage.jpg`: band wide shot on the left (back left, back right, keys), lead close-up top right, sax bottom right. Each face gets a dashed amber ring slot with a small "+" badge at the bottom right; slot coordinates are `SEATS` in `tool.tsx` and must be re-measured if the image changes. The stage follows the hero switch: on "Made here" or "Dogs" it fades in `stage-ours.jpg` / `stage-dogs.jpg` (three frames from the output at the same moments and crop, same slot coordinates), so users can see who each seat became. The output versions sit on layers above and are preloaded, so only the opacity changes and the background is never swapped abruptly.
  - The five seats start with the faces of the five dogs from the dog version (every competitor shows people, so dogs set us apart); at least one photo of your own is needed to order. Tap a musician to upload their photo; the face then sits on that musician with a small × at the top right: on your photo it puts the dog back, on a dog it keeps the original coach. Swapped faces can be dragged to another seat (an empty seat moves it, a filled seat swaps them; the target grows and gets a cream and amber ring; dropping outside the stage cancels). On desktop an image file can be dropped straight onto a musician: the seat under the pointer highlights the same way, and releasing uploads to that seat. Names are short mono labels (Lead, Sax, Keys, Back left, Back right).
  - Under the stage, one amber mono status line that updates live with uploads: `swapped 2/5 · whole video in one go · $9.98`.
  - Above the stage, three small presets (Just me on lead / Whole band / Back row only) that only highlight seats and open the picker for the first empty one; nothing is forced.
  - Below the stage, a small note "tap a dog to swap in your photo, × puts the dog back, × on a dog keeps the coach"; a consent box "I have the rights to these photos, everyone is 18+, this is a parody"; the price right on the button.
  - Mobile works the same way, tapping people on the stage, with a minimum face size and short names so labels don't overlap.
- **Generating**: three steps of copy in turn (`Setting the stage` → `Swapping in your lead` → `Final mix`) with an estimated wait in mono. No meaningless percentages.
- **Result**: the output card has two download buttons (with the song, silent) and a line: if TikTok mutes it, post the silent version and add "Outstanding" from the sound library.
- **Pricing cards**: three tiers side by side, the featured one with an amber outline and an Anton tag, the other two on dark panels. Below the price, "Before you pay": the AI result looks like the person but not identical; failures refund credits automatically; one-time payment, no subscription.
- **Sample captions**: under the card, "Made from an AI-generated photo" or "Reference clip, not a live result".
- **Crew line-up**: not equal-sized cards but a gig-poster line-up: one crew per row, big Anton name (amber on odd rows), a one-line description on the right, thin dividers between rows.
- **Content pages** (tutorial, template, guides): long poster-paper cards, `#efe6d2` with a faint halftone, ink text, serif headings, Anton tags, mono tables.
